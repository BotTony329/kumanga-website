WHY KUMANGA EXISTS

Most AI comics tools generate a finished page and leave you no way to change it. Kumanga generates reusable assets — characters, poses, expressions, backgrounds — and gives you a real non-destructive editor to compose them into pages you control.

Move a character without regenerating the panel. Reframe a full-body asset into a close-up with zero new generations. Rewrite one line of dialogue without touching the art. That's what a harness is: the AI is powerful, and you hold the reins.

WHAT'S INSIDE

A complete studio, not a prompt box

Everything below is already built and running in the v0.1 open-source preview.

Reusable Characters

Create a character once, use them across every panel. Structured pose & expression libraries, reference-aware generation, and semantic slot switching keep them consistent page after page.

Scene & Asset Library

Backgrounds, props, uploads, screentones, speed lines, focus lines and impact bursts — all organized, all drag-to-panel, all yours to reuse in any project.

Manga Editor

An infinite canvas with polygon panels you can reshape vertex by vertex, classic layouts (including yonkoma), viewport framing from full-body to close-up, and full undo/redo.

Manga Agent

Tell it what you want in plain language — "make them blush in panel 2" — and it plans, reuses what exists, generates only what's missing, and commits a verified, fully editable result.

Local-First & BYOK

Projects and images live on your machine, not our cloud. Connect any compatible AI provider with your own key — Gemini, OpenAI-compatible, or a custom endpoint. No account, ever.

Export & Share

High-quality PNG export straight from the live scene graph at 1× or 2×, with provenance metadata on every generated asset. Your pages leave the studio ready to publish.

HOW THE HARNESS WORKS

Natural language in, an editable page out

The Manga Agent treats the AI as a creative director — but code owns the state, the identity of every asset, and the final commit. Nothing lands on your page unverified.

1

You direct

Describe the scene, the shot, the mood, the dialogue — in your own words. Your exact wording is locked as evidence before any AI sees it.

2

The agent plans

A single creative-director pass maps your intent to cameras, interactions and bubbles — validated against a strict contract, never guessed IDs.

3

Reuse before generate

Existing characters and assets are reused or transformed first; new images are generated only for what's genuinely missing. Your tokens are respected.

4

Verified commit

A deterministic verifier checks every actor is placed and dialogue is byte-exact before committing. The run lands whole — or rolls back cleanly.
